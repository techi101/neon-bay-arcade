How it works

Nothing to download

The city is built at runtime from a seeded grid — buildings, palms, neon and ramps are all placed in code. There are no model or texture files in this repo.

Sound from an oscillator

Engine note, siren and impacts are synthesized live with the Web Audio API. Engine pitch tracks your actual wheel speed.

Run it locally

Clone the repo and serve the folder: npx serve . then open the printed address. That is the whole setup.
